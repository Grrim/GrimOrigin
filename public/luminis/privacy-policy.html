Privacy Policy for LUMINIS: Song of the Stars

Last Updated: November 13, 2026

GRIM ORIGIN STUDIO ("we", "our", or "us") operates the mobile game LUMINIS: Song of the Stars (the "Application", package: com.grimorigin.luminis). We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing a safe, transparent gaming experience.

Summary: LUMINIS is an offline-first puzzle and relaxation game. We do not collect, sell, or share any personal data. Your game progress, unlocked constellations, and preferences are stored strictly on your local device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

LUMINIS does not collect or request any Personally Identifiable Information (PII), including but not limited to:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses
  • Precise or approximate GPS location data
  • Contact lists, photos, microphone, or camera access
  • Financial, billing, or payment information (the game is 100% free with no in-app purchases)
  • Biometric or health data

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide core game functionality, LUMINIS saves the following data locally on your device via standard local storage APIs:

  • Game Progress: Completed chapters, unlocked celestial spheres, stardust balance, and high scores.
  • User Settings: Audio volume, high contrast mode, and language preference.

This data remains on your device and is never uploaded to any remote server or third party.

3. Third-Party Services and SDKs

LUMINIS is designed with privacy at its core:

  • No Advertising SDKs: There are no third-party advertising SDKs or tracking networks (no Google AdMob, Unity Ads, etc.).
  • No Third-Party Analytics: We do not track player activity across other apps or websites.

4. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA / GDPR-K)

LUMINIS is not directed at children under 13. The Application does not knowingly collect, transmit, or share any personal information from any user, including children.

5. Permissions Requested

The Application requests only the minimal permissions required for Android runtime:

  • Vibration / Haptic: Used exclusively for tactile feedback during orb interactions (can be toggled in settings).
  • Notifications (Optional): Used locally on device for daily ritual reminders. No personal data is transmitted.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all progress data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over it at all times. Uninstalling the Application or clearing its storage in your Android device settings will permanently delete all local game data.
